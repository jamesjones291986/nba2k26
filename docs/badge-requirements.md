# NBA 2K26 Badge Requirements — Attribute Minimums by Tier

All badges require meeting attribute thresholds to unlock each tier. Badges with "/" between attributes mean EITHER attribute qualifies. Badges with "+" mean BOTH are required.

> Source: nba2klab.com, gamerant.com, destructoid.com (cross-referenced)

---

| Badge | Attribute | Bronze | Silver | Gold | HoF | Legend | Height |
|-------|-----------|--------|--------|------|-----|--------|--------|
| **Deadeye** | Mid-Range Shot | 73 | 85 | 92 | 95 | 99 | Any |
| **Deadeye** | Three-Point Shot | 73 | 85 | 92 | 95 | 99 | Any |
| **Limitless Range** | Three-Point Shot | 83 | 89 | 93 | 96 | 99 | Any |
| **Mini Marksman** | Mid-Range Shot | 71 | 82 | 94 | 97 | 99 | ≤ 6'3 |
| **Mini Marksman** | Three-Point Shot | 71 | 82 | 94 | 97 | 99 | ≤ 6'3 |

| **Set Shot Specialist** | Three-Point Shot | 65 | 78 | 89 | 95 | 98 | Any |
| **Shifty Shooter** | Mid-Range Shot | 76 | 87 | 91 | 96 | 99 | ≤ 6'11 |
| **Shifty Shooter** | Three-Point Shot | 76 | 87 | 91 | 96 | 99 | ≤ 6'11 |

- Deadeye, Mini Marksman, Set Shot Specialist, Shifty Shooter: Either Mid-Range OR Three-Point qualifies

- Limitless Range is Three-Point only
